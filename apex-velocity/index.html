Apex Velocity — Privacy Policy

Updated 1 November 2026 · Developer: Sujan Mukherjee

Offline game

Apex Velocity is a single-player motorcycle racing game. The Android version bundles its game code, images and sounds for offline use. It requires no account and includes no advertising, analytics, purchases or third-party tracking SDKs. It requests no camera, microphone, location, contacts or storage permissions and has no Internet permission.

Information stored on your device

Race records, earned game credits, selected rider, career progression, settings and acknowledgement of the privacy notice are stored locally. They are not transmitted to the developer. We do not collect, share or sell personal information through the app.

Your controls and deletion

Clear Apex Velocity app storage in Android Settings or uninstall the app to delete local records. Android backup is disabled. There is no cloud sync or account recovery.

Children and device services

The same no-collection practices apply to all players, including children. The game uses Android System WebView to render its content. Device and app-store services may process information independently under their own policies.

Contact and policy updates

Questions about this policy or privacy rights can be sent to sujan99india@gmail.com. If you contact us by email, your email address and message are used to respond to your request. Do not send sensitive information. This page will be updated if the app’s data practices change.
