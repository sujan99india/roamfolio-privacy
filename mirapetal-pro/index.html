MiraPetal Pro Privacy Policy

Version 1.2.0 · Updated 10 October 2026
Developer: Sujan Mukherjee
Android package: com.sujanmukherjee.mirapetal.pro

On-device creative workspace

Photo editing, Portrait Stories and Portrait Session Desk operate locally. The app has no Internet permission, account, analytics, ads, cloud processing, facial recognition or automated identity analysis. Imported photos and edits are not sent to the developer.

Permissions

Camera is optional and requested when you enable it. Microphone is optional and requested only for video with sound. Android’s system picker grants access only to photos you select. System save and share dialogs handle chosen exports; no broad storage or photo-library permission is requested.

Drafts and saved sessions

Photo draft retention is optional and off by default. If enabled, one original, edited image and settings are saved locally. Color presets are stored locally. Portrait Session Desk holds up to six working images in memory and lets you explicitly Save session to this device. One saved session contains compressed photo previews, project title, brief, notes, ratings, crop position and template choices in private IndexedDB. Saving replaces the prior saved session. Delete saved session removes it; New session clears the active workspace only. Clearing app data or uninstalling removes all private drafts, sessions and presets. Android automatic backup is disabled.

Exports and sharing

Image exports are re-encoded and do not copy source GPS metadata. Contact sheets include the session title, image labels, ratings and notes you choose. Review these before sharing. Android Share grants temporary read-only access to the chosen export. The recipient service has its own policies. Saved or shared files remain outside the app and must be removed separately.

Temporary files

The app prepares exports in private cache. Save preparation files are removed on completion or cancellation. Files prepared for sharing can remain until the next launch after 24 hours or until you clear cache. Video contains sound only if you enabled it.

Payment and sample content

Pro uses a one-time paid store download, with no subscription or in-app billing. The store handles payment; the app receives no payment-card details. Sample portraits depict fictional adults and were created with AI. Manual tools do not identify people or infer personal traits.

Your choices and privacy requests

You can view and remove local information in the app or through Android Settings → Apps → Storage → Clear data. Uninstalling also removes private app data. The developer cannot remotely access or restore it. For questions, access, correction or deletion requests concerning support correspondence, email sujan999india@gmail.com. Do not send unnecessary sensitive information.

Support and this website

If you email support, the developer receives your email address and message through the email provider to respond to you. This is separate from the offline app. This public policy page has no advertising, tracking scripts or contact form. GitHub Pages handles ordinary network requests needed to serve the page under its own privacy practices.

Policy updates

This policy applies to the version and package above. Changes to information handling will be reflected in an updated policy.
