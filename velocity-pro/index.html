VELOCITY PRO

Privacy Policy & Support

Effective date: 5 November 2026. Developer: Sujan Mukherjee. Android package: com.sujanmukherjee.velocitypro.

VELOCITY PRO is an offline arcade motorcycle racing game with six motorcycles, six courses and three difficulty levels.

Information handled by the game

The app does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal information. It has no accounts, advertisements, analytics, tracking, purchases or third-party advertising SDKs. All game assets are bundled and gameplay requires no internet connection.

Local storage

Your motorcycle and track choices, difficulty and best times are saved in app storage on your device. The first-launch notice acknowledgement is also saved locally. This data is not sent to the developer. Clear app storage in Android Settings or uninstall the app to remove it. The app disables Android backup.

Permissions and children

The app requests no sensitive device permissions and no internet permission. It does not access contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or advertising identifiers. It does not knowingly collect information from children or any other players.

Support

Email sujan999india@gmail.com for support or privacy questions. If you email us, your email address and the information you choose to send will be used to respond to your request. Do not send passwords or other sensitive information. You may request deletion of support correspondence by email, subject to any retention obligations.

Store and policy website

The app store and this policy website operate separately from the offline game and may process technical information under their own privacy policies. This page is hosted by GitHub Pages.

Updates

This policy will be updated if the app's data practices change. Check the effective date for the latest version.
