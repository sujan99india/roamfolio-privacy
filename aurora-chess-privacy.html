Aurora Chess Privacy Policy

Effective date: 4 November 2026

Developer: Sujan Mukherjee. Android package: com.sujanmukherjee.aurorachess.

Offline game

Aurora Chess is an offline chess game. The Android app does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal information. It has no accounts, advertising, analytics, tracking, online chat or in-app purchases. Computer moves are calculated on your device. No generative AI service is used.

Data stored on your device

The app stores your current game, move history, clocks, selected difficulty, game mode, theme preference and privacy-notice acknowledgement locally. This data stays on your device. Android backup is disabled. Starting a new game replaces the saved game. Clear app storage in Android Settings or uninstall the app to remove its local data.

Exporting a game

PGN export occurs only when you select it. The Android document picker lets you choose a destination. An exported file contains chess-game information and remains wherever you choose to save it. If you choose a cloud provider, that provider handles the file under its own policy. Delete exported files using your file manager.

Permissions and third parties

The app requests no sensitive device permissions and includes no advertising or analytics SDK. It does not access contacts, location, camera or microphone. Bundled Android game assets do not require a network connection. Huawei AppGallery and Android may independently process installation, device or store information under their own policies. This policy concerns the Android app; visiting this page is subject to the hosting provider GitHub's privacy policy.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users. There are no public profiles, messaging or social-sharing services in the app.

Your choices and contact

No developer-held user database exists to access, correct or delete. You control local data and exported files as described above. For questions or privacy requests, contact sujan99india@gmail.com. If you email us, your email address and message will be used to respond and deleted when no longer needed for that purpose, except where retention is required.

Updates

Material changes to data handling will be described in an updated policy and, where needed, in the app before changed handling begins.
