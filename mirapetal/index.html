MiraPetal Privacy Policy

Free edition 1.2.0 · Updated 12 October 2026
Developer: Sujan Mukherjee
Android package: com.sujanmukherjee.mirapetal

中文摘要

Local photo workspace

MiraPetal processes selected photos, camera frames, edits and Product Set projects on your device. It has no Internet permission, account, ads, analytics, cloud processing or facial-recognition service. Photos and product information are not sent to the developer.

Optional permissions

Camera permission is requested when you enable capture. Microphone permission is requested only when you enable sound for video. Android’s system photo picker grants access to photos you choose. The app does not request broad photo-library or storage permission. Editing imported photos and building Product Sets do not require camera or microphone permission.

Product Set projects

The open product workspace is held in memory. Choosing Save project stores one project in private on-device IndexedDB: up to four compressed JPEG working copies, product name, SKU or collection, view notes, canvas style and per-view framing. Working copies have a maximum 2048-pixel longest side. Saving replaces the previous saved product project. Open saved project restores it. New product clears only the open workspace. Delete saved project removes the saved project and leaves the current open workspace available. The developer cannot remotely access or restore these projects.

Editor drafts and other local data

Remember my last edit is optional and off by default. Enabling it saves one photo draft and its edits locally until you disable retention or choose Clear saved draft. Settings and any saved editor looks are held locally. Android automatic app backup is disabled. Clearing app data or uninstalling removes private projects, drafts and settings.

Exports and sharing

You choose where an export is saved using the Android system save dialog. Product comparison boards contain your product title, SKU and view notes; review them before sharing. Image exports are re-encoded without copying source GPS metadata. Video records sound only when you enable the microphone. If you choose Share, the chosen recipient service handles the exported file under its own practices. Exported files outside the app remain until you delete them at their destination.

Temporary files

The app prepares exports in its private cache. Save preparation files are removed when the save completes or is cancelled. Share files can remain until the next launch after 24 hours or until you clear the cache.

Free edition and samples

MiraPetal Free has no subscription or in-app billing. Product Set is included in the free edition. Some existing editing tools describe a separate paid Pro edition; any store purchase is handled by the store. Product Set samples are original illustrations, labeled as a demo. Sample portraits depict fictional adults and were created with AI. Framing, makeup and retouching are manual tools; the app does not identify people, measure product size or automatically remove image backgrounds.

Your choices and support

Use Delete saved project in Product Set, Clear saved draft in settings, or Android Settings → Apps → Storage → Clear data to remove local data. For questions or requests about support correspondence, contact sujan999india@gmail.com. If you email support, the developer receives your email address and message through the email provider to respond to you. Do not include unnecessary sensitive information.

This policy website

This page has no tracking scripts, advertising or contact form. GitHub Pages handles the network requests needed to serve it under its own privacy practices. Visiting the page is separate from the offline app. Changes to information handling will be reflected in an updated policy.
