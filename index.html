ROAMFOLIO PRIVACY POLICY
Version 1.0.3 · Last updated 8 October 2026
Developer: SUJAN MUKHERJEE
Contact: worldviewhub@gmail.com
Android package: com.sujanmukherjee.roamfolio

Roamfolio is an offline travel organiser. This policy describes the Android build supplied with this release.

1. Information stored locally
The app stores trip names, destinations, travel dates, traveller counts, itineraries, packing entries, expenses, notes and appearance preferences in its private local application storage. These fields are provided by you. There is no user account, cloud database or developer-operated server.

2. No developer collection or tracking
The app does not transmit personal information to the developer. It contains no advertising, analytics or payment SDK. The Android package requests no internet, location, microphone, camera, contacts or storage permissions. It does not provide travel booking, live transport schedules, currency-rate lookup or map navigation.

3. Backups you choose
Export backup uses Android's system document picker to write an unencrypted JSON file to a location you select. Import reads only the JSON file you choose through the picker. The app does not browse or scan your other files. It does not retain broad filesystem permissions.
If you choose a cloud storage provider, email a backup or share it through another service, that service's own policies apply. Roamfolio does not receive the exported file. Store your backups privately. Do not enter passwords, passport numbers, full payment-card numbers or other sensitive credentials in notes.

4. Retention and deletion
Local information remains until you edit or delete it, clear the app's data or uninstall. Android automatic app backup is disabled. Exported files remain in the location you selected and must be deleted separately there. Keep a backup before uninstalling if you want to retain your plans.

5. Choices and access
You can view, edit and remove your trip information within the app and export a copy. Because the developer does not receive or store your local information, the developer cannot retrieve or restore it for you. Contact the developer with questions; do not attach sensitive trip or identity information unnecessarily.

6. Children and example content
Roamfolio is a general-purpose travel organiser. It is not designed to collect information from children or operate an account-based social service. The included example itinerary and amounts are labeled illustrative content and can be removed.

7. Updates
A later version that changes these practices should publish an updated notice. This policy applies only to the features and package described above.

8. First-launch notice
Before the Android app opens your saved trips, it displays a native privacy notice with Read policy, Continue and Exit. The full policy is bundled in English and Simplified Chinese and can be read offline. Choosing Continue stores only a local acknowledgment of this policy version. It does not enable collection or sharing. After clearing app data, reinstalling without retained data, or a policy-version change, the notice appears again.

9. Privacy website and support
The public policy website is separate from the offline app. Its hosting provider processes ordinary web requests. If you contact support by email, the developer receives your email address and the information you choose to send, and uses it to respond. Do not include sensitive information unnecessarily.

ROAMFOLIO 隐私政策
版本 1.0.3 · 更新日期：2026年10月8日
开发者：SUJAN MUKHERJEE
联系邮箱：worldviewhub@gmail.com
Android 应用包名：com.sujanmukherjee.roamfolio

Roamfolio 是一款离线旅行计划管理应用。本政策说明本次发布的 Android 版本如何处理信息。

1. 仅在本机保存的信息
应用在其私有本地存储空间中保存旅行名称、目的地、旅行日期、同行人数、每日行程、行李清单、费用记录、备注和外观偏好。这些内容由您主动输入。应用不设用户账户、云端数据库或开发者运营的服务器。

2. 开发者不收集或跟踪您的应用数据
应用不会将个人信息传输给开发者，也不包含广告、分析统计或支付 SDK。Android 安装包不申请联网、定位、麦克风、相机、通讯录或存储权限。应用不提供旅行预订、实时交通时刻查询、汇率查询或地图导航。

3. 由您选择的备份
导出备份时，应用通过 Android 系统文件选择器将未加密的 JSON 文件保存到您选择的位置。导入时，只读取您通过系统选择器选中的 JSON 文件。应用不会浏览或扫描您的其他文件，也不会获取或保留广泛的文件系统访问权限。
如果您选择云存储服务、通过电子邮件发送备份，或使用其他服务分享备份，相应服务的隐私政策将适用。Roamfolio 开发者不会收到您导出的文件。请妥善保管备份，不要在备注中输入密码、护照号码、完整银行卡号码或其他敏感凭据。

4. 保存期限与删除
本地信息会保留到您修改或删除它、清除应用数据或卸载应用时。应用已禁用 Android 自动应用备份。已导出的文件仍保存在您选择的位置，您需要在该位置另行删除。如需保留旅行计划，请在卸载前导出备份。

5. 您的选择与访问
您可以在应用内查看、修改、删除旅行信息，并导出副本。由于开发者不会接收或保存您的本地信息，因此无法代您读取或恢复这些信息。如有问题，请通过联系邮箱咨询；请勿在邮件中附上不必要的敏感旅行信息或身份信息。

6. 儿童与示例内容
Roamfolio 是通用的旅行计划管理工具，不以收集儿童信息或提供基于账户的社交服务为目的。应用内置的示例行程和金额均标注为演示内容，您可以删除。

7. 政策更新
如果后续版本改变上述信息处理方式，将发布更新后的隐私说明。本政策仅适用于上述功能及应用包。

8. 首次启动隐私提示
Android 应用读取旅行数据前，会显示原生隐私提示，提供「阅读政策」「继续」「退出」选项。完整政策以英文和简体中文随应用提供，可离线阅读。点击「继续」只在本机保存您已看到当前政策版本的记录，不会开启数据收集或共享。清除应用数据、重新安装且未保留数据、或政策版本更新后，将再次显示提示。

9. 隐私政策网站与支持
公开的政策网站独立于离线应用，网站托管方会处理普通网络请求。若您通过电子邮件联系支持，开发者会收到您的邮箱地址及您主动发送的信息，仅用于回复问题。请勿发送不必要的敏感信息。
