Bloom Privacy Policy

Effective 23 October 2026 • Policy version 2026-10-23 App: Bloom - Pregnancy Companion Android package: com.sujanmukherjee.bloom_companion Developer: Sujan Mukherjee Contact: worldviewhub@gmail.com

Scope

This policy describes the local-first Android release 0.2.1. Account, AI and family-sharing services are not configured in this release. No subscription or payment is required. Bloom is a wellness journal, not a medical device or emergency service.

Information on your device

Information you choose to enter can include your name, due date, pregnancy dates, journal entries, symptoms, mood, weight, hydration, appointments, kick and contraction records, checklists and selected photo/report copies. Entries and imported photo copies are stored in an encrypted local AES-GCM vault; encryption keys use Android secure storage. Android automatic app backup is disabled. A compromised or unlocked device may still expose information.

Collection and sharing

This release has no advertising or developer-operated analytics service and does not automatically upload your journal to the developer. Google ML Kit, used for optional report OCR, collects diagnostic and usage information: device and app details, per-installation identifiers, performance, API configuration, feature-event and error information. These data can be sent to Google over HTTPS. Image inputs and recognized text are processed on the device and are not sent to Google by ML Kit. Google handles SDK data under its privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). Its published SDK disclosures are at https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure. Account, AI and family-sharing features are unavailable. Firebase libraries are included for possible future connected services but are not configured or initialized with a project in this release. A future release enabling them must provide updated information and obtain applicable consent before sending your information.

Features and permissions you choose

Photo selection reads the items you select using Android's picker. Report text recognition uses Google ML Kit on the device. Notification permission is requested only for reminders; reminder notifications use generic text. The optional home-screen widget displays pregnancy week when you enable it. PDF print/share, email, maps, telephone and website actions open system or third-party services at your request. Those providers receive the information you choose to share and apply their own policies. Exported reports may contain sensitive health information.

Retention, access and deletion

Your local records remain until you delete them, clear app data or uninstall. You can view and remove entries and clear your journal from the You section. Manage original photos and exported PDFs separately: deleting journal records does not delete copies you have exported or shared. There is no developer-held journal backup or recovery service. Uninstalling or losing the device may permanently lose your records.

Privacy choices

At launch, before the main app starts, Bloom asks you to read this policy. Read privacy policy opens the bundled full text without internet access. Accept and continue stores this policy version only on this device; Decline and exit closes the app without opening the journal. Clearing app data or changing the policy version causes the prompt to appear again. The policy is also available from You and the connected-care screen. Acceptance does not activate cloud sharing or grant optional device permissions.

Children and support

Bloom is not directed to children under 13. Do not enter another person's private information without appropriate permission. If you email support, the developer receives your email address and the information you send and uses it to respond. Avoid sending health records or other sensitive information unnecessarily. Contact worldviewhub@gmail.com for privacy or deletion questions about support correspondence.

Policy website

The public policy page is hosted on GitHub Pages. Its hosting provider processes ordinary website requests, including IP address and browser request information, under its own privacy policy. Opening the bundled in-app policy does not contact this website.

Changes

If information handling changes, this policy will be updated and the app will present the new policy version. Contact worldviewhub@gmail.com with questions.
