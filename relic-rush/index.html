Privacy policy

Effective 12 October 2026 · Android version 1.0.2
Package: com.sujanmukherjee.relicrush

简体中文

Offline play. Device-local progress.

Relic Rush is an offline running game provided by Sujan Mukherjee. It does not send gameplay, motion readings or personal information to the developer. This Android build has no network permission and does not require an internet connection.

Information processed on your device

The game stores coins, gems, selected and unlocked characters, upgrades, mission progress, daily challenge progress, best distance, local scores and their dates, and sound, control and graphics settings in the app’s local storage. This enables progress to continue between sessions. There is no user account, cloud synchronization or online leaderboard.

The Android wrapper can read device orientation while the app is active to support optional tilt controls. The game uses those readings for steering only when tilt is enabled. Readings are processed in memory and are not saved or sent off the device. Touch and keyboard input are also processed locally.

Collection, sharing and bundled software

The game does not transmit names, email addresses, location, contacts, photos, microphone recordings, advertising identifiers, analytics events or crash reports to the developer. It does not sell or share player information. The bundled Three.js graphics library renders locally; no external game assets are downloaded. There are no advertising, analytics, payment, account or social-network SDKs in this version.

Advertisements and purchases

Ad screens, rewarded revives and shop purchases labeled as demonstrations are simulations. No real advertisement is requested, no payment is processed and no payment information is collected. Coins and gems are game items with no cash value.

Your choices, retention and deletion

You can play with swipe or on-screen controls without enabling tilt. Progress and settings remain on your device until cleared. To erase them, open Android Settings → Apps → Relic Rush → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the app; labels vary by device. Clearing data permanently resets progress. The developer has no remote copy to retrieve or delete. Android system app backup is disabled for this build.

Children

The game does not request age or identity information and does not collect personal information from children through gameplay. A parent or guardian may contact the support address about information voluntarily provided in an email.

Support and privacy requests

Contact worldviewhub@gmail.com. If you choose to email support, your address and the information you provide are handled through email services to respond to your inquiry. Do not include passwords, payment details or unnecessary sensitive information. You may request access, correction or deletion of information you supplied in support correspondence, subject to applicable requirements. Support correspondence is retained only as needed to address the inquiry and applicable obligations. Gameplay data remains on your device.

Store and policy website

Huawei AppGallery and the device operating system handle store and installation information under their own privacy notices. This policy website is hosted separately on GitHub Pages. Visiting it makes an ordinary web request; the hosting provider may process connection data such as IP address and request time. The developer has added no analytics scripts, forms or tracking to this page.

Changes

If future versions introduce online features, real advertising, purchases or other data processing, this policy and relevant app disclosures will be updated before release. The effective date above identifies this version.

Relic Rush 是离线跑酷游戏，不向开发者发送游戏数据、设备方向读数或个人信息。本版本没有网络权限，无需联网。

设备上的信息处理

游戏在本地保存金币、宝石、选择及解锁的角色、升级、任务和每日挑战进度、最佳距离、本地分数与日期，以及声音、控制和画质设置。没有账号、云同步或在线排行榜。

应用在运行时可读取设备方向，以支持可选的倾斜控制。只有启用倾斜控制后，游戏才使用读数进行转向。读数仅在内存中处理，不保存或发送到设备之外。触摸和键盘输入也在本地处理。

收集与共享

游戏不会向开发者发送姓名、邮箱、位置、通讯录、照片、录音、广告标识符、分析事件或崩溃报告，不出售或共享玩家信息。内置 Three.js 在本地渲染，不下载外部游戏资源。本版本没有广告、分析、支付、账号或社交 SDK。

广告与购买演示

标注为演示的广告画面、奖励复活和商店购买是模拟功能，不请求真实广告，不进行付款，也不收集支付信息。金币和宝石没有现金价值。

选择、保存与删除

您可以不启用倾斜控制，改用滑动或屏幕按钮。进度和设置保存在设备上，直到被清除。如需删除，请进入 Android 设置 → 应用 → Relic Rush → 存储 → 清除数据，或卸载应用；具体名称因设备而异。清除数据会重置进度。开发者没有远程副本。本版本禁用 Android 系统应用备份。
