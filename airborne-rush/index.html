AIRBORNE RUSH

Privacy policy

Android app · Versions 0.3.2–0.3.3 · Updated 12 October 2026

Airborne Rush plays offline. Gameplay data stays on your device; the app does not send it to the developer.

Who this policy covers

This policy describes the Android app Airborne Rush, provided by Sujan Mukherjee, with package name com.sujanmukherjee.airbornerush. It applies to the app version described above. App stores, your device operating system and websites have their own privacy practices.

Information used by the game

The app does not ask you to register, sign in or provide personal details. It does not collect or transmit your name, email, phone number, contacts, precise location, advertising identifier, photos, microphone recordings or camera images. There are no advertising, analytics or remote crash-reporting services embedded in this version.

To keep your progress between sessions, the game saves earned credits, selected cars, upgrades, paint choices, career stars, control and sound preferences, and best-race ghost replay data on your device. Ghost replay data consists of race timing and virtual-car positions, not recordings of you. These saves are used only to restore progress, apply preferences and replay your own race.

Connections and permissions

The Android app has no Internet permission and blocks network loads in its game viewer. Game code, artwork and audio are bundled with the app. It does not request camera, microphone, contacts, location or access to your personal files. Supported steering and controller inputs are processed locally. If you choose the website or email link in the privacy screen, your device opens a separate browser or email app. That external app uses its own permissions and privacy practices; no game saves are attached or sent.

Sharing and purchases

This version does not transmit gameplay or device data to the developer or to advertising or analytics providers. It does not sell data, provide online accounts or chat, or include in-app purchases. The app store and device platform may separately process download, security or diagnostic information under their own policies.

Your saved data and choices

Local progress remains until the app's storage is cleared or the app is uninstalled. To remove it, open your phone's Settings, find Airborne Rush under Apps, select Storage, and choose Clear data or Clear storage. Menu names vary by device. This resets progress, upgrades, preferences and ghost replays. App-managed cloud backup is disabled, and the developer cannot recover erased saves.

There is no online account to delete and no server-side gameplay profile for the developer to access or copy. You can change sound and control preferences within the game. For questions about access, correction or deletion of information you voluntarily send to support, email worldviewhub@gmail.com.

Children

The same offline data practices apply to all players. The app does not ask children for personal details or enable messaging between players. Its store age rating concerns racing and vehicle-impact content; it is not a request for a player's date of birth.

Contact

Support email: worldviewhub@gmail.com.

If you choose to email support, your email provider and the developer receive the information you send. Send only what is needed to explain your issue; do not include passwords, payment details or sensitive documents. Support correspondence is separate from the offline game's local storage. It is used to respond to your request and is retained only as needed for that purpose and any applicable record-keeping obligations. You can request access, correction or deletion by emailing the same address.

This policy website

The policy page itself uses no added analytics, advertising scripts, forms or tracking cookies. Visiting the hosted page requires a normal web connection, so GitHub Pages, its hosting provider, may process technical request information such as IP address and browser details to deliver and protect the page. This is separate from offline gameplay.

Changes

The policy will be updated when the app's data practices change. Check the version and date shown at the top. Any future online, advertising or account features will need an updated disclosure before release.
