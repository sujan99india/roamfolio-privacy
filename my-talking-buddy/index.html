My Talking Buddy Privacy Policy

Version 1.0.1 · Updated 10 October 2026
Developer: Sujan Mukherjee
Android package: com.sujanmukherjee.mytalkingbuddy.hw

Offline play

The full game is included in the APK and loads from local assets. It does not have Internet permission, a user account, an advertising SDK, an analytics SDK or a payment SDK. Your progress is not transmitted to the developer.

Local progress

Pet nickname, needs, coins, rewards, preferences, unlocked items and game scores are stored locally on this device. Choose a pet nickname without personal information. Android automatic backup is disabled. Data remains until you clear app data or uninstall.

Optional microphone

Only when you choose the voice feature does the app request microphone permission. It records a short clip of up to six seconds and plays it back locally at a playful pitch. Audio is temporary, is not uploaded or saved as a recording file, and is released after playback. Recording stops when the app is put in the background. Refusing permission does not prevent other play. Revoke access in Android Settings, Apps, My Talking Buddy, Permissions, Microphone.

Children and purchases

No real name, contact details, photos, location or account information is requested. A parent or guardian can manage microphone access and clear local progress. Any prototype coin/ad demonstrations do not charge money or contact advertising services. There is no real in-app billing.

Your choices and privacy requests

You can view and remove local information in the app or through Android Settings → Apps → Storage → Clear data. Uninstalling also removes private app data. The developer cannot remotely access or restore it. For questions, access, correction or deletion requests concerning support correspondence, email worldviewhub@gmail.com. Do not send unnecessary sensitive information.

Support and this website

If you email support, the developer receives your email address and message through the email provider to respond to you. This is separate from the offline app. This public policy page has no advertising, tracking scripts or contact form. GitHub Pages handles ordinary network requests needed to serve the page under its own privacy practices.

Policy updates

This policy applies to the version and package above. Changes to information handling will be reflected in an updated policy.
