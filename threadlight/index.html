Threadlight Privacy Policy

Versions 1.0.0 and 1.0.1 · Updated 10 October 2026
Developer: Sujan Mukherjee
Android packages: com.sujanmukherjee.threadlight (original) and com.sujanmukherjee.threadlight.hw (Huawei correction)

Offline game

Threadlight is an offline puzzle game. It has no user account, Internet permission, advertising, analytics, payment SDK or cloud service. The developer does not receive your gameplay data.

Local progress

Completed puzzles, stars, active puzzle state and preferences are saved only in the app’s private local storage. Android automatic backup is disabled. Data remains until you clear app data or uninstall. The developer cannot restore it.

Permissions and purchases

This build requests no camera, microphone, location, contacts or shared-storage permissions. Any paid download is handled by the app store under its policies. The game has no in-app purchases and receives no payment-card information.

Children

The game does not collect personal information from players of any age and has no chat, accounts or user-generated content.

Your choices and privacy requests

You can view and remove local information in the app or through Android Settings → Apps → Storage → Clear data. Uninstalling also removes private app data. The developer cannot remotely access or restore it. For questions, access, correction or deletion requests concerning support correspondence, email sujan999india@gmail.com. Do not send unnecessary sensitive information.

Support and this website

If you email support, the developer receives your email address and message through the email provider to respond to you. This is separate from the offline app. This public policy page has no advertising, tracking scripts or contact form. GitHub Pages handles ordinary network requests needed to serve the page under its own privacy practices.

Policy updates

This policy applies to the version and package above. Changes to information handling will be reflected in an updated policy.
