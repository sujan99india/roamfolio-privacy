Run Temple Pro — Privacy Policy
Effective date: 31 October 2026
Developer: Sujan Mukherjee

Run Temple Pro is a single-player jungle runner. This policy describes the Android release of the game.

Information stored on your device
The game stores your best distance, selected character and colourway in the app's local WebView storage. It also stores whether you have acknowledged the first-launch privacy notice. These records are used only to remember your progress and settings. They are not sent to the developer.

No account or tracking
No account or sign-in is required. This release contains no advertising SDK, analytics SDK, social login, in-app purchase or behavioural tracking. The developer does not collect your name, email, contacts, location, photos, microphone recordings or device advertising identifier through the game.

Bundled content and network access
Game code, models, artwork and sounds are bundled in the Android app. A virtual HTTPS address is handled inside the app to load these local files. The Android Internet permission supports this WebView mechanism; this release blocks external web requests from the game. No online leaderboard or cloud saving is provided.

System software and the store
Android, the device manufacturer, the installed system WebView and AppGallery may handle information under their own policies. Their services are not operated by the developer.

Retention and deletion
Local progress remains until you choose Reset progress in the game menu, clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app. Reset progress removes the saved best distance and character choice. Clearing Android app storage or uninstalling also removes the first-launch notice preference. Android backup is disabled for this app.

Children
The game does not request personal information from children or provide user-to-user communication. The AppGallery age rating will describe the game's fantasy-adventure content.

Security
External pages and resource requests are blocked by the embedded game. No camera, location, contacts, microphone or external-storage permission is requested.

Changes and contact
If future releases add online services or change data handling, this policy and the in-app disclosures will be updated before those features are used. For privacy questions, contact the developer at sujan99india@gmail.com.
