Privacy policy

Effective 12 October 2026 · Android version 1.1.1
Package: com.sujanmukherjee.canopyrelay

简体中文

Your progress stays on your device.

Canopy Relay is an offline running game provided by Sujan Mukherjee. This Android build has no account system, advertising, analytics, real-money purchases, online leaderboard or developer-operated game server. It requests no Android permissions and does not require an internet connection.

Information used by the game

The game stores scores, distance, earned crystals, unlocked couriers, upgrades, mission and delivery progress, local daily results, selected options and settings on your device. A limited in-game position trace displays your personal-best echo. This is a gameplay recording, not your physical location. Touch and keyboard input are used locally for control.

Collection, sharing and third-party software

The game does not transmit your gameplay or personal information to the developer. It does not collect names, email addresses, device location, contacts, photos, microphone recordings, advertising identifiers or analytics events. It does not sell or share player information. The bundled Three.js renderer, character assets and fonts operate locally. No external assets are downloaded at startup.

Daily courses and in-game currency

Daily courses use the date set on your device. Daily results and scores remain local and are not worldwide rankings. Couriers and upgrades use crystals earned through play. No real money is charged, and crystals have no cash value.

Storage, retention and deletion

Progress remains in the app’s local storage until you erase it. In the game, open Settings → Clear local progress and confirm the reset. You may also clear app storage through Android Settings or uninstall the app. Android system app backup is disabled. There is no account to delete and no developer-held copy of game progress to retrieve.

Children

The game does not collect personal information from players, including children. A parent or guardian may contact the support address about information voluntarily provided in support correspondence.

Store, policy website and support

Huawei AppGallery and your device platform handle installation and store-related information under their own privacy notices. This public policy is hosted separately on GitHub Pages. Opening it makes an ordinary web request; the hosting provider may process connection information such as IP address and request time. This page includes no analytics scripts, forms or tracking added by the developer.

If you choose to email support, the developer receives the address and information you include and uses it to respond. Support correspondence is retained only as needed to handle the inquiry and applicable obligations. You can request access, correction or deletion of information you supplied in that correspondence. Do not send passwords or unnecessary sensitive information.

Contact and changes

Support and privacy enquiries: sujan999india@gmail.com. If a future version changes the game’s data practices, this policy and the relevant app disclosures will be updated before that version is distributed.

Canopy Relay 是一款离线跑酷游戏。本版本没有账号、广告、分析服务、真实货币购买、在线排行榜或开发者运营的游戏服务器。不申请 Android 权限，也无需联网。

本地处理的信息

游戏在您的设备上保存分数、距离、游戏水晶、已解锁角色、升级、任务与递送进度、每日成绩、选项和设置。有限的游戏内位置轨迹用于显示个人最佳成绩的虚影，不是您的真实位置。触摸和键盘输入仅在本地用于控制游戏。

收集与共享

游戏不会向开发者发送游戏数据或个人信息，不收集姓名、邮箱、设备位置、通讯录、照片、录音、广告标识符或分析事件，也不会出售或共享玩家信息。内置的 Three.js 渲染库、角色资源和字体均在本地运行；启动时不会下载外部资源。

每日路线与水晶

每日路线根据设备日期生成。成绩仅保存在本地，不是全球排名。角色和升级使用游玩所得水晶，无需支付真实货币，水晶没有现金价值。

保存期限与删除

游戏进度保存在设备上，直到您删除它。在游戏中进入 Settings（设置）→ Clear local progress（清除本地进度）并确认重置，也可在 Android 设置中清除应用数据或卸载应用。本版本禁用 Android 系统应用备份。没有需要删除的账号，开发者也没有可以恢复的远程进度副本。

儿童

游戏不收集任何玩家（包括儿童）的个人信息。对于主动通过支持邮件提供的信息，家长或监护人可联系下方邮箱。
