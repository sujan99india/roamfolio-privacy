Crowd Forge: Robot Rush

Privacy policy · Version 2.0.0 · 31 October 2026
Developer: Sujan Mukherjee

Offline by design

This version operates entirely offline. It has no user accounts, advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, in-app purchases or network permission. The game does not collect or transmit personal information.

Local game data

Campaign progress, virtual coins, upgrades, selected units and settings are saved in private application storage on your device. Android cloud backup is disabled for this app. No cloud synchronization is provided.

Permissions

The app requests no camera, microphone, location, contacts, shared-media or network permissions.

Deleting progress

Use your device’s app settings → Storage → Clear data to delete saved game progress. Uninstalling the app removes its local application data. Local data can also be cleared through your device's application settings.

Children and purchases

This version does not collect information from any player and offers no real-money purchases, advertising or player-to-player communication.

Platform services

Downloading the app through an app store is governed separately by that platform's privacy practices. This policy describes the game itself.

Contact and changes

For support or privacy questions, use the developer contact shown on this game's official store listing. Any future version that introduces data collection or online services will provide an updated policy before those services are enabled.
